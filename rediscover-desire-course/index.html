FAQs

What if I don't have a partner?

This course is designed for any woman who wants to reconnect with her desire. You don't need to be in a relationship for that to happen — in fact, if you're not in one, you'll have even more time and space to pay attention to what genuinely turns you on.

What if I'm busy?

All the practices and reflections in this course take between 15 and 30 minutes a day. Some can be done at any point in your day and take no more than 2-3 minutes. What matters most is prioritizing pleasure and yourself for these 10 days — less about finding time, more about a shift in mindset.

Would I always have access to the community?

Yes — purchasing the course gives you lifetime access to all the practices and to our women's online community.

What if something opens up and I'm having a very intense experience?

I'm here for you if something intense comes up or you need support. Message me and I'll help, or you can book an additional session directly so we can work through whatever triggered it together.

Can I also have personal support through the experience?

Yes. You can purchase the course plus book a private session with me at a discounted rate (€50), exclusively for course participants. Book an intro session here for a first short conversation.

Do I have to finish all 10 days together?

It's ideal to go through the course as it's designed — 10 consecutive days, each one building on the last. That said, it's not the end of the world if you can't, and you can always pick it back up later. Move at your own pace and the intensity that's right for you right now.
