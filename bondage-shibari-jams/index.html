Bondage Jams

A space for you to tie with others, hang out and learn some shibari basics

🥨 A bondage jam is a peer learning and social hangout event where we learn from each other and do rope in a relaxed atmosphere. You can hang out, have a drink and chat with people if you don't feel like tying. Being there is enough! 🥨

This is not an educational event. The exchange of knowledge and skills happens naturally, in conversation and between all participants. There is a one hour intro lesson on basic knots and safety in ropes at the beginning of the event. If you would like to learn more, you can take part in some of my shibari workshops or book a private class with me.

Important details

Who is this event for: Everyone with interest and/or experience in shibari

Language: English/Bulgarian

What to bring: yourself, optionally ropes (I can also lend you some ropes)

What to wear: comfortable clothes

Solo or partnered: both, this is a peer-learning event and partners are not necessary

Format: Your role as the participant is to share (if you desire to) your knowledge and to get inspired by other people. You can ask for help from other participants and help others whenever needed.

Price: 20 euro for the class and the jam

Please do not be late, as I open the event and go through the rules at the beginning. If you feel socially awkward, like many of us do, bring someone you trust with you, or send me a message in advance so I can support you.
