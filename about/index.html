Learn More About Me

I have been doing adult sex education in one form or another for the past 4 years. It started when I moved from New York to Berlin, got sober, and discovered BDSM. Berlin — a city well known for its sex positivity and freedom of sexual expression — provided me with the opportunity to explore my own sexuality. I discovered and enjoyed different forms and types of relationships, kinky and not-so-kinky practices, different perspectives and ideas around sexuality.

While in Berlin I started sluttish.us — a site focusing on female sexuality. It gave me the chance to connect with people who had knowledge and experience in the fields of consensual non-monogamy, BDSM, performance art, sex and kink education, feminist and indie porn, sex shop owners etc. It gave me the sex education I never formally received, and so much more. Running sluttish was what convinced me to pursue a career connected to sex, intimacy, and relationships.

Shortly after my mother passed away, COVID started and I moved back to Bulgaria. While here, I started my education with Somatica Institute in San Francisco — a coaching modality, focused on embodiment, sexuality, and communication.

Training with Somatica, as well as being sober and going through my own internal processes in the past 4 years made me finally ready to face my own trauma and to start dealing with all the defense mechanisms, reactions, triggers and responses that got shaped from it. I participated in multiple trauma and emotional response trainings, including Dance and Movement Therapy and Somatic Embodiment & Regulation Strategies. At this point, I feel ready to start seeing and helping people go through their own inhibitions and hurt, and discover their own sexual and emotional authenticity and completeness.

I am based in Sofia, Bulgaria, but I mostly see my clients online.
