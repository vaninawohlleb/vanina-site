Private Shibari Lessons

Come explore rope in your own pace and learn shibari

Private Shibari lessons for individuals and couples who want to begin their rope journey in a focused, supportive space.

These sessions cover the fundamentals — safety, basic ties, communication, and body awareness and manipulation — while moving at your own pace. Beyond technique, we also explore presence, intimacy and the emotional dynamics that rope can awaken.

Whether you're completely new or wanting a more personalized introduction, private lessons allow for depth, clarity, and embodied learning from the very beginning.

What to expect

Private lessons are structured yet intuitive. We begin with a short conversation about your experience level, intentions, boundaries, and curiosities. From there, we move into hands-on practice — learning foundational knots and ties, understanding safety principles, and building confidence step by step.

You can expect clear guidance, practical demonstrations, and real-time feedback. We move slowly, prioritizing communication, consent, and body awareness. There is space to ask questions, pause, adjust, and explore what feels aligned for you.

The details

Duration: 1 hour

Location: Private, comfortable space

Lessons happen by appointment only and require our preliminary conversation first. I bring over five years of shibari practice and respect for the trust you're placing in me.
