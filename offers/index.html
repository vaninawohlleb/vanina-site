Coaching

As a sex and intimacy coach, I offer packages designed to help you break free from shame, reconnect with your desires, and create a more fulfilling erotic life. At the moment I am working only with shadow desires, desires within a couple that are untold, and any kink/BDSM curiosity you might have. Through a mix of shadow work, authentic communication, and embodiment techniques, we'll work to dissolve performance anxiety, reignite your passion, and deepen your connection — to yourself, your desires, and your partner.

I believe the best results come from two approaches: regular practice with tools you can incorporate into your life, and commitment. I recommend exploring the package options below and reaching out if you have any questions or need guidance.

If you have a question, or you are coming with something outside of the topics of the packages, you can book a single session. Individual sessions are €80 (one hour) and couple's sessions are €130 (an hour and a half). If you're in Bulgaria and able to attend in-person sessions, check out the Bulgarian version of the site for local pricing. You can easily book a free intro session here.
